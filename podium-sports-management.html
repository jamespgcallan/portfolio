A player-first social media identity built around strong photography, editorial typography and a consistent approach to football representation.

Personal design project by James Callan. Not commissioned by or affiliated with Podium Sports Management. Photographs referenced in the original presentation belong to their respective creators.

01 / Creative direction

Built around the players.

The concept looks at how a football representation agency can create a more recognisable social presence without losing the individual character of the players it represents. The proposed system uses clear layouts, bold announcements and editorial touches for player features, international recognition and career milestones.

02 / Visual identity

Colour & typography.

Archivo Black carries the major headlines and key announcements, while Cormorant Garamond adds an editorial feel to spotlight pieces. Deep green, bright green and warm off-white provide a consistent palette across the designs.
