A little more about me

Football business, communications and the people behind the game.

I’m James Callan, based in Dublin, and my work sits across football business, communications, media and marketing. I’m interested in what happens away from the pitch as much as what happens on it: how clubs are run, how they reach supporters and how they build something that lasts.

I’ve written for RTÉ Sport, contributed to club media and built independent football projects including Touchline Sport and LOI Talk. At Boardroom Ball, I moved from writer to leading the newsletter, working across editorial planning, production and audience development. My work has included matchday content, brand concepts, communications and football business reporting.

Alongside football media, I work in the legal sector and hold a Business & Law degree from TU Dublin. I’m now studying for an MSc in Business Intelligence & Digital Marketing at Brunel University London. I also coach and scout football. The aim is to bring the football knowledge and the business thinking together, rather than pick one and leave the other behind.
