01 / Social media growthBohemians Academy

Featured project

Academy content.
8,000+ followers.

Academy football deserves proper coverage. During my involvement with Bohemians Academy, its Instagram following grew from zero to more than 8,000 in less than three months.

My contribution covered content ideas, creative graphics and ongoing publishing support. Academy football has strong stories, but it takes consistent coverage to bring them to an audience beyond the players and their families.

Period: academy launch and first three months of the account. Reported account growth during the period I was involved. This is an account-level milestone, not a claim that I achieved it alone.

The editorial brief

Objective
Build a football business newsletter that readers would return to throughout the week.
My role
Writer, then newsletter editorial lead from January to April 2026.
Strategy & execution
Plan and produce three editions a week, maintain a consistent editorial voice and cover the financial and operational side of football. Worked around commercial partner exposure including Classic Football Shirts and Champions Travel.
Results
